## 8. Working style \+ collaboration

**How I run meetings** Always with a clear agenda prepared and sent to participants beforehand. Always with a target outcome or decision in mind. I'm an active listener - I let the room speak before I do. Notes (or the doc itself) capture decisions, action items, owners, and dates, and I send them out the same day. I keep meetings as short as they need to be and cancel them when they no longer have a clear purpose.

**How I write docs** Short, structured, and opinionated. TL;DR / executive summary up top so the reader can get the answer in 30 seconds. Decisions over discussions - what was decided, by whom, when, and why. Links to source data rather than restating it. I tend to write in headers, bullets, and tables rather than long prose.

**How I handle disagreement** Direct but kind. I surface the tradeoff rather than the position - "if we do X we lose Y" - and try to get us debating the tradeoff rather than each other. If we're stuck, I push for a decision-maker or a clear timebox. I'm willing to escalate when needed, and equally willing to disagree and commit if the call has been made.

**How I prioritise when 5 things are on fire** Impact x reversibility, fast. What blocks the most other work or has the highest cost-of-delay goes first; reversible things wait. I escalate what can't be done with current capacity rather than silently absorbing it, and I'm ruthless about pausing or cutting anything that doesn't directly serve the top one or two outcomes.

**How I run a 1:1** 30 mins, monthly minimum, weekly if useful. Structure is light: their agenda first, then blockers, then growth / feedback / career, then anything I need to land. Less status reporting, more "what's actually getting in the way" and "what would help most."

**Async vs. sync default** Default async - written updates, clear docs, decisions captured in writing. Sync only for decisions, real blockers, ambiguous problems, or anything emotional / sensitive. I think a lot of "let's just jump on a call" can be a 4-line Slack message.
